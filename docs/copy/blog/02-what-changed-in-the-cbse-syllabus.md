**The largest change is that the middle stage is being taught out of new books with new names, so the chapter list a parent remembers no longer matches the book in the bag, and neither does any list written for the previous books.**

Everything below is read off the board's own documents. We fetched them on 3 September 2026, re-fetched every one of them on 5 September 2026, and each came back byte for byte identical, so what follows is what those files say and not what anybody remembers them saying. Where our own reading of a document is still unsettled, that is said too.

## CBSE publishes a curriculum document for one stage and prescribes books for the other

This is the fact that explains most of the confusion, and it is not a change, it is just rarely stated. For the secondary stage CBSE publishes a curriculum document per subject per year, on its own site. For the middle stage it does not: it prescribes the NCERT textbook, and the textbook is the syllabus.

So "the CBSE syllabus" means two different kinds of source depending on which class you are asking about, and presenting both as one tidy list flattens something real.

## The middle stage: new books, new names, new shape

The prescribed NCERT books for the middle stage have been replaced, and the new ones are not renamed editions of the old. Mathematics is now Ganita Prakash. Science is now Curiosity. Social science is now Exploring Society: India and Beyond, and it is organised as themes rather than as three separate books for history, geography and civics.

The contents pages are on ncert.nic.in, in each book's own prelims PDF. Ganita Prakash for the class 8 mathematics course is published in two parts, and its parts are the units, with the book's chapters underneath them. Exploring Society runs on lettered themes, so a chapter now sits under something like Governance and Democracy or Economic Life Around Us rather than under a subject name.

What this means in practice, for a parent: chapter-wise notes and solutions written for the previous books will not line up. Not slightly. The chapter names are different, the order is different, and the grouping is different. If a chapter list you are working from still reads the way it did two years ago, that is the tell, whoever wrote it.

## Reading trap one: what class 10 science actually examines

The class 10 science document for 2026-27 is Science, Class X, Secondary Curriculum Part 1, on cbseacademic.nic.in, and the course structure sits on page 4 with the syllabus prose running to page 6.

Inside it, three areas sit in boxes the document marks as assessed only formatively: Periodic Classification of Elements, Evolution, and Motor, Electromagnetic Induction and Electric Generator. Everything else is examined. The Note for Teachers on page 6 names exactly what is not.

We got this wrong on the first pass, and the reason is worth repeating because it will catch anybody reading the same file. The PDF's text layer indents one whole page, and our first reading took that indent for the document's formative box, which produced three topics marked as not examined that the document does in fact examine. Reading the rendered pages instead of the extracted text fixed it. The document also carries one genuine contradiction of its own, between its prose and its Note for Teachers, on Heredity, and our file records the contradiction rather than picking a side.

## Reading trap two: marks that belong to a group, not a unit

In the class 12 physics document the course structure table prints marks against a bracketed group of units, not against each unit inside it. Read the table quickly and you will copy one unit's number onto the wrong row, which is exactly what our own first reading did, twice.

The class 11 mathematics document carries a related note in the board's own words: there is no chapter-wise weightage inside a unit. So a per-chapter marks figure for that course is not in the document, and if you are holding one it came from somewhere the board did not write.

## What is still open, on our side

Our class 11 mathematics file is marked provisional, because the document numbers three chapters under Coordinate Geometry and our reading holds two. That is our error, not the board's, and the file says so rather than quietly rounding up.

ICSE and ISC are in the same honest position for a different reason. We hold their unit lists, checked against the board's own booklet, and we do not hold a topic layer under those units, so our pages for them carry the unit and the provenance and nothing invented underneath it.

## How to check any of this yourself

Open the board's own file, find the page, and read the sentence. Every claim above names the document and the page it is on precisely so that you can do that in about ten minutes and disagree with us if we are wrong. That is the whole point of citing a source rather than asserting a fact.
