What helps us answer faster

  • The board and year, if it is about a lesson or a chapter.
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • A screenshot, if something looked wrong on screen.
  • The email the account uses, if you have one. Never a password.

If a child seems at risk, or Wobo said something it should not have, write and say so plainly. It reaches the same inbox and it is read.
