# Streaks, and taking a day off

**A streak counts the days you showed up. Missing one is fine, and Wobo will never threaten you with losing it.**

**How it works**

A day counts when you do something real: a lesson, a practice run, a boss. Asking Wobo one question counts too. Your day rolls over overnight, on your own clock.

**Rest is part of it**

Every week includes a rest day that holds your streak without you doing anything. Use it or do not. Wobo will sometimes suggest resting, and when Wobo does, it is a real suggestion, not a trick to get you back tomorrow.

**What you will never see from us**

No countdown timer. No message telling you what you are about to lose. No badge taken away for being ill, or busy, or on holiday. If you disappear for a month, Wobo is exactly as pleased to see you when you come back.

**Milestones**

At 7, 30, 100 and 365 days, Wobo draws you something. That is the whole reward, plus the XP.

**Turning it off**

If a streak is the kind of thing that makes you anxious rather than steady, turn it off in settings. Nothing else about Wobo changes and your progress is unaffected.
