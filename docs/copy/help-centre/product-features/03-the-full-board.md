# The full board

**Inside a lesson, the board is the whole screen, and the lesson happens on it.**

There is no page of text with a video at the top. A lesson is Wobo drawing, one idea at a time, while you act on what is drawn.

**How a lesson runs**

- One idea per screen. Something to tap, drag, shade or build before it moves on.
- Wobo draws and talks together, and pauses when there is something for you to do.
- Interrupt whenever you like. Tap, type or speak and the pen stops mid-stroke.
- Ask anything mid-lesson. Wobo answers on the same board and picks up where it stopped.
- A lesson closes with a boss battle, then the XP.

**Scrubbing back**

The board has a timeline. Drag back to watch a step redraw itself, exactly as it was drawn the first time. Useful when you looked away at the wrong moment.

**Leaving**

Leave whenever you like. Your place is saved, down to the step you were on, and nothing is lost. We never take progress away for stopping.

**Wobo while you work**

Wobo contracts to the orb in a corner and watches. Get three things wrong the same way, or sit still for a while, and Wobo offers a pointer rather than an answer. If you would rather be left alone, set Wobo to quiet on You, under Settings.
