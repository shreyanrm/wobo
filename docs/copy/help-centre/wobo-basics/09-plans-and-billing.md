# Plans and billing

**Wobo is free to use every day. Pro and Max raise the daily allowance for exam season, and the price is the same for everyone.**

**Free**
A real product, not a trial. The whole tutor: your board, your lessons, the drawn board, the films, the models you can drag, practice, boss battles, your progress and the parent link. What is limited is how many turns you get each day. The counter is on screen and tells you the exact time it resets.

**Pro**
Five times the free allowance, and Wobo reads answers aloud in your accent. [Owner: nothing else is named here until it is decided and built. Any further Pro benefit, and whether there is a family plan and how many seats it carries, is an open decision recorded in `docs/copy/README.md`.]

**Max**
Twenty times the free allowance, so nobody counts, and two learners on one plan. For the board year.

No plan unlocks the teacher. Every subject and every form is in Free; what a plan buys is room to ask more in a day.

**What "turns" means**
One turn is one thing you ask Wobo, and everything Wobo draws, plays, says or sets for you in reply. [Owner: exactly which actions count against the daily allowance is an open decision. The budget meter counts every tier, so nothing is described here as free of charge until the metering rule is settled.]

**Paying**
Card, or the payment method your device offers. At checkout you confirm two things in writing: that you accept the terms and the privacy notice, and that you understand the amount, when it renews and how to cancel. You should never be surprised by a charge.

**Renewals**
We write to you 7 days before any renewal, and 30 days before as well if you are on an annual or family plan, with the amount and the date. Cancel any time before it and you are not charged.

**Cancelling**
Settings, then your plan, then cancel. Two taps: the control, and one confirmation that says what happens. No offer to stay, no reason to give, no survey. You keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for, nothing renews after that, and the account goes back to free with everything you have learnt intact. Change your mind before that date and one tap puts the plan back.

**Money back**
We do not give money back as a gesture of goodwill. Cancelling is the answer instead: you keep the plan until the period you paid for ends and nothing renews. Where the law gives you a refund you still have it, and the cancellation document lists every case.

**Receipts**
Every payment gets one by email. For anything that looks wrong, write to [support email] with it.
