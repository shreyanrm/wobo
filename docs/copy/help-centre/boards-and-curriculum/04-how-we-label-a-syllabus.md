# How we label a syllabus

**Every syllabus in Wobo says where it came from and how far we have checked it, in plain words, on the page.**

We would rather tell you a thing is uncertain than let you find out in an exam.

**The four labels**

- **Official, verified.** From the board's own published document, checked by us, and confirmed. It names the board and the academic year: "Official CBSE 2026-27, verified".
- **Found on the board's site, still checking.** The structure came from a real official document and passed our automatic checks, but nobody has confirmed it by hand yet. Usable, and worth a glance against your own book.
- **Shared by another learner, not yet checked.** Someone offered their school's syllabus. Treat it as a starting point.
- **Drafted from your syllabus, check it.** Built from what you gave Wobo.

**Seeing the source**

Every label opens the source: the document, and the page or section each chapter came from. If a chapter has no source, it is not there.

**How something becomes verified**

The checks have to pass, and then either two different learners use it without correcting its structure, or we review it ourselves.

**If a label is wrong**

Write to support@heywobo.com and name the syllabus and the label. There is no flag control in Wobo yet, so the mailbox is the route, and it is read by a person.
