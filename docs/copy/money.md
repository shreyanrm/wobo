# The money's voice: the lines, verbatim

Written once, used word for word on every surface where money is mentioned (docs/SELL.md, "The money's
voice"). One line per surface. Never two on one screen.

| Surface | The line |
|---|---|
| plans page, above the plans | Your plan pays for the drawings, the voice, and the next lesson. That is where the money goes. |
| checkout, beside the price | This is what keeps Wobo drawing for your child, and for the next one. |
| the bar on You, under the day's allowance | Today's lessons are paid for by your plan. Thank you for that. |
| receipt mail, first line after the amount | Thank you. This pays for the drawings, the voice, and a better lesson tomorrow. |
| plan opened mail | Thank you for choosing a plan. It pays for the drawings, the voice, and the next child's first lesson. |
| renewal mail | Your plan renewed. Thank you for keeping the drawings coming. |
| failed payment mail | The payment did not go through. Nothing changes today. When you are ready, the plan picks up where it left off. |
| parent's pay screen | What you pay here becomes drawings, a voice, and lessons that keep going until the topic is understood. |
| donate screen | A gift here goes to a child whose family cannot pay. It becomes their drawings and their lessons. |
| free learner, once, on You | Thank you for learning with Wobo. A plan adds more lessons a day and pays for the drawings that make them. |

Forbidden on any money surface: subscription, billing cycle, tier, upgrade, premium, pro features,
unlock, limited offer, exclusive, appreciate your business, mission. The word "business" never appears.
