# Taking the name: how Wobo becomes the answer to "Wobo"

**The owner, 2026-09-09:** *"We need to be more dominating. We need to take over the name Wobo."*

Today three products share the name. **wobo.ai** is a job-search app with an App Store listing,
Trustpilot reviews and a Product Hunt page. **wobo.app** sells digital workbooks. We are **heywobo.com**,
which is the weakest domain of the three. Ask an engine about "Wobo AI tutor" and it answers about the
job app, and one engine says outright that Wobo AI is not an AI tutor.

This is winnable, and faster than it looks, because entity resolution is not a global fact. It is
per-market and per-query, and it follows the people.

## 1. The strategy in one line

**Do not fight for the bare word first. Win every qualified query and the whole Indian market, and
the bare word follows the volume.**

Nobody wins "wobo" by asking. An engine decides what a name means by what people who type it go on to
click, in their market. "Wobo" in the United States means a job app. "Wobo" in India, once fifty
thousand students type it and land on us, means us. A job-search app in another country has no way to
stop that. So the fight is not for the word. It is for India, and it is for these queries first:

    wobo ai tutor · wobo app · wobo cbse · wobo class 10 · wobo icse · wobo tutor india
    wobo login · wobo download · heywobo · wobo drawing tutor · wobo doubt solver

Every one of those is unclaimed today. Winning all of them is a month of the work already planned, and
each one teaches the engines a little more about which Wobo is which.

## 2. The domains

Checked on 2026-09-09. `wobo.com`, `wobo.in`, `wobo.ai`, `wobo.app` and `wobo.io` are taken. Two of
the available ones are worth having, and one of them is better than the name we own:

| Domain | Why |
|---|---|
| **wobo.ink** | We are the tutor that draws. The name and the product are the same word. It is short, memorable, and no other Wobo can ever use it as well as we can. Worth taking as the brand's second home and possibly its first. |
| **wobo.co.in** | Entity resolution is per-market, and this is the strongest available signal that Wobo is Indian. Cheap, and it makes "wobo india" ours by default. |

Also free and worth parking so nobody else in education takes them: `wobo.education`, `wobo.study`,
`wobo.school`, `wobo.academy`, `wobo.co`, `wobo.net`, `wobo.me`. The whole set costs less than a
month of hosting.

**The recommendation:** register `wobo.ink` and `wobo.co.in` now, park the education set, and keep
`heywobo.com` as the live site until a rename is deliberate. If `wobo.ink` becomes the primary later,
that is a redirect and a rebuild of the entity work, so decide it once rather than drift into it.

## 3. Own the whole first page

For "wobo" and every qualified query, ten results should be ours. Each is a listing we create with the
identical copy in docs/copy/press-kit.md:

1. heywobo.com, with the site pre-rendered so it can actually be read
2. heywobo.com/press
3. The Play Store listing, named Wobo (an app store entry is one of the strongest entity signals there is)
4. The App Store listing
5. The YouTube channel, named Wobo, with the drawn explanations
6. The LinkedIn company page
7. Crunchbase
8. Product Hunt
9. Wikidata (this is the item an engine reads to decide the name refers to a thing)
10. The chapter pages, which will out-number everything the other two have

## 4. The signals that actually move an engine

- **Brand searches.** The strongest signal there is, and the one we control. Every piece of content,
  every video, every classroom poster asks people to **search Wobo**, not to click a link. A hundred
  people typing the name is worth more than a thousand clicking a URL.
- **One sentence, everywhere, unchanged.** Sameness across sources is how a machine becomes confident.
- **A `sameAs` graph.** The site's Organization markup lists every profile we own, so an engine can
  walk from the site to the app store to Wikidata and see one entity.
- **Say the qualifier until it is not needed.** Everything we publish says "Wobo, the AI tutor",
  never a bare "Wobo", until the engines have learned it. Then we drop it.
- **A trademark.** File "Wobo" in India in class 41 (education) and class 9 (software). It is the
  legal backstop, it is cheap, and if either of the others moves into education it is the only thing
  that matters. This is an owner action with a lawyer.
- **Press and awards** (docs/GROWTH-PRESS.md), which exist mostly to create the independent sources
  that make the above credible.

## 5. What we never do

Buy ads on the other two names. Mention them anywhere. Attempt to take their domains. Register
lookalikes of their names. The fight is won by being the thing people mean, not by attacking anyone.

## 6. The measure

Monthly, on the growth desk: the position of heywobo.com for "wobo" and each qualified query in India;
the number of first-page results we own; whether Google shows a knowledge panel for Wobo and what it
says; and a standing prompt to each answer engine asking what Wobo is, with the answer recorded. The
day all five engines answer "an AI tutor for Indian school students" without a qualifier in the
question, the name is ours.
