# Settings

**Everything you can change about Wobo lives in one place, reached from the quiet "you" affordance in the top left.**

**Account**
Your name, your phone number and email address, adding a second way to sign in, setting a password, and signing out.

**Appearance**
Auto, light or dark. Auto follows your device. In light, Wobo draws like marker on paper; in dark, like chalk on slate.

**Motion**
Reduce motion, on, off or auto. With it on, everything happens in the same order and with the same timing, it just arrives without the movement. Auto follows your device.

**Sound and voice**
Narration on or off, sound effects on or off, and Wobo's accent. Narration and sound are separate, so you can have a silent Wobo who still draws. On an adult account, hands-free listening is here too, off until you turn it on. [Owner: hands-free is an open decision; the plan specifies hold-to-talk and a hotkey only.]

**Language**
The language Wobo teaches in. Your board's own names for things are kept alongside the English.

**Learning**
How much Wobo interrupts: quiet, balanced or proactive. On quiet, Wobo speaks up only when you ask. On proactive, Wobo offers a pointer when you have been stuck for a while. Streaks on or off is here as well, for anyone who would rather not count days.

**Notifications**
Email and push. Progress moments, weekly summaries and exam reminders each have their own switch, and festival greetings have a switch of their own separate from other progress mail. Every message we send says how to turn that category off. Billing and account messages are not switchable, because they are the record of what you paid and who you are.

**The parent link**
Create, view or revoke the weekly summary link for a parent, and set how often it goes out.

**Data and privacy**
What Wobo remembers, consent, exporting everything, and deleting your account.

**Your plan**
Which plan you are on, what its day carries, the date it runs to, and Cancel. Cancelling takes two taps and a plan already ending can be put back in one. [Owner: there is no billing history in the product and no turns-left count on this screen; the daily allowance appears where you are asking, not in settings. Neither is described here until it is built.]
