# The design award submissions

What goes into the forms, field by field, filled in by hand. Nothing in the repo submits it.
docs/GROWTH-PRESS.md §1 and §2.3 are the plan: the design awards are judged on craft, a submission
is ours to make, and it is made the week the site is static and fast. Every line a juror reads is in
a quote, and `press.test.ts` holds those quotes to the kit and to the copy law.

## When it may go

- **The public pages are pre-rendered** (done: `apps/web-pwa/scripts/prerender.ts`) **and fast.**
  Measure the home page and one chapter page at 390 and at 1440, in both themes, on the live site,
  the same week. A slow page does not get submitted to a craft award.
- **The ink is good** (GROWTH-PRESS §1). The drawn answer is the reason a juror stops; it has to
  land every time.
- **The fee is the owner's call.** Awwwards, CSS Design Awards and FWA charge to submit. The
  galleries below do not.

## Where

In this order: **Awwwards** (Site of the Day), **CSS Design Awards**, **FWA**, then the galleries,
which take the address and a line: **Godly**, **Land-book**, **Httpster**. Each accepted entry is a
link from a site engines trust, and it is claimed nowhere until it exists: a badge goes on the site
only after the award does.

## Design statement

The field every one of these forms has under some name ("about the project", "concept"). It
describes the site, which is what is being judged, and it says only what a juror can see for
themselves on the page.

> Wobo is a tutor that answers a question by drawing it, so the site is made the way the product
> works: ink on white paper, and colour only where it has a job.

> Every drawing on the page is drawn stroke by stroke as you reach it, in the same handwriting the
> tutor writes in, once, and then it stays where it landed.

> The drawing is one part. The same question is answered four ways as you scroll: drawn, filmed,
> handed over to drag, and said out loud.

> One blue does the pointing, one yellow is the highlighter, green confirms and coral marks what
> needs care. The night theme is drawn on its own rather than inverted, and with reduced motion switched
> on, every drawing arrives already finished.

> Every page is written out in full before it is sent, so it reads the same with scripts switched
> off.
