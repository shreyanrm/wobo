# Help centre

The help centre at heywobo.com/help. Three groups, illustrated, searchable. Wobo answers help questions from this content first, so a learner rarely needs to open a page at all; when they do, the page must be short enough to read standing up.

**Rules for every article**
- The first line is a complete answer on its own. A reader who stops there is not stranded.
- Under 250 words after that first line.
- Sentence case, no emoji, no exclamation marks.
- Wobo by name; they and them only when a pronoun is unavoidable; Wobo speaks as "I".
- No provider, model or vendor names. Ever.
- Every illustration is Wobo drawing the thing the article is about, in ink, on white or slate.
