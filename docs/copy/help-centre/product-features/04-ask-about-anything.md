# Ask Wobo about anything on the screen

**Circle it, hold on it, or select it, then ask. Wobo already knows what it is.**

This works on every screen in the app: a lesson, a simulation, a graph, a paused video, a settings page, your own progress chart.

**Three ways to point**

- **Circle it.** Drag a loop around it with your finger, mouse or stylus. The loop fades and Wobo has it.
- **Hold on it.** Press and hold for a moment on a phone, or hover and hold on a laptop.
- **Select it.** Select text the normal way and ask about the selection.

Then ask, by voice or by typing. "Why is this one negative." "What is that arrow for." "Explain this bit."

**Why the answers are exact**

Wobo does not look at a picture of your screen and guess. Every diagram, slider and simulation in Wobo reports what it is currently showing, so when you circle a corner of a graph Wobo has the actual values, the actual equation, and what you last changed.

**Things worth asking**

- "Explain this."
- "Show me where I went wrong."
- "Say this in terms of football."
- "Why is this wrong?" after a wrong answer.
- "Read it aloud."
- "Teach it back to me": you explain, Wobo listens and finds the hole.

**Video and pictures**

Pause one of our videos and circle any part of the frame; Wobo annotates it exactly. For something we did not make, Wobo reads the region you circled and says plainly that it is reading it rather than knowing it.
