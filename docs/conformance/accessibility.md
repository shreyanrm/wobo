# Conformance register — Accessibility (WCAG 2.2, Level AA)

**Product:** Wobo (web PWA at `apps/web-pwa`, public site + signed-in app)
**Register written:** 2026-09-04
**Branch:** `the-life`
**Target:** WCAG 2.2 Level AA (every Level A and Level AA success criterion, plus a product-specific
section for the board and the drawn answers)

> **The one rule for this register.** Nothing is ticked without evidence. Every row carries either a
> `file:line`, or a command and its actual output. Where a claim needs a live service, a real
> assistive technology, or a human, it is marked **NOT VERIFIED** and the register says exactly what
> would verify it. This repo has previously claimed certifications it never had; this file adds
> nothing to that list.

## 9. The gaps that would actually hurt, worst first

1. **A67 — the board does not describe what it draws.** Five of eight objects on a real Pythagoras board are announced as nothing. For a product whose premise is that Wobo *draws* the explanation, a learner who cannot see it gets the labels and not the picture. Everything needed to fix it already exists in `packages/wobo/src/board/schema.ts`; `spokenLabel` (`renderer.tsx:674-690`) just stops early.
2. **A17 — the primary call to action fails contrast in night, on nine pages.** `color:#fff` hard-coded over `--pig` gives 2.97:1. One token swap.
3. **A14 / A15 / A16 / A18 — the white ground made the muted greys and the accents unsafe.** `--ink-3` at 3.38:1 carries crumbs, tags, placeholders and meta across the whole app; rose emphasis is 2.80:1; marigold-on-ink in night is 1.40:1. This is 100+ failing nodes in one axe pass.
4. **A78 — the "High contrast" setting does not touch the tokens the app now paints with.** A learner who notices problem 3 and reaches for the fix gets nothing. That is worse than not shipping the setting.
5. **A23 — the public site's focus ring is 1.75:1 in light mode.** Every keyboard visitor to the marketing site, the plans page and the legal pages has a focus indicator they cannot reliably see.
6. **A48 / A47 — two new WCAG 2.2 criteria fail.** Targets under 24 px (the wordmark on every document page, "Sign in", the `/you` crumb button), and drag-only operation of the ordering answer and the board plane's handles.
7. **A37 — the command palette is a modal that is not one.** Focus walks out of it, focus is not returned on close, and the page behind is neither inert nor hidden while it declares `aria-modal="true"`. The correct pattern is already in the repo twice.
8. **A36 / A40 — no page titles and no `h1` on most app screens.** `/learn` and `/parent` have no headings at all; every app route's tab says only "Wobo".
9. **A81 — the reduced-motion override for smooth scrolling loses a specificity fight** and has therefore never worked.
10. **A85 — the 404 has no `main` landmark.**

None of the above is architectural. The board layer, the answer layer and the token layer were all built with accessibility in mind — the answers module in particular is better than most shipped products. What has happened is that the move to a white ground revalued every grey, and a set of small, individually cheap defects has accumulated on top of a sound structure.
