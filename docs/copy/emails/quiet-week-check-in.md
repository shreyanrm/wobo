## Rules
- **No guilt and no fake concern.** Not "we miss you", not "your studies are slipping", not a count of days away.
- Never mention an exam here unless one is genuinely in the calendar, in which case `before-exam` handles it instead.
- Never send to an account under 18 during a school holiday period known for their region.
- One send only. A learner who wants to be away is allowed to be away.
