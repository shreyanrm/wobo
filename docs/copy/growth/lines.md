# Lines the owner wants on the public pages, and how they are set

## "Learning can be extremely fun and engaging" (owner, 2026-09-08)

The intent is right and it belongs on the public pages. The sentence as written is not how we say it,
for three reasons SELL.md and voice.md already hold: "can be" hedges a promise; "extremely" is the
intensifier every advert uses; "fun and engaging" is the category's own cliché, so it says nothing a
parent has not read on ten other sites. The same intent, said the way we say things, with a picture
behind each:

1. **"Learning, the fun kind."** Short enough for an eyebrow or a section head. Beside the climb, a
   bonus level and a boss.
2. **"Some of it is a game. All of it counts."** For the section that shows the arcade and the XP;
   it makes the promise and the honesty in one breath.
3. **"You will forget you are studying."** The boldest; for the landing's second chapter, beside a
   drawn answer landing on a page. It is puffery in the legal sense (docs/CLAIMS.md) and it is the
   line a fourteen-year-old would repeat.

Where: the landing page's chapter on the climb (one of 1 or 3), the for-parents page under the
"two modes" section (2), the plans page's eyebrow stays "Every subject · every board · free every day".
One line per section, never all three on one page (SELL.md: one job per page). The hero stays "Fall
in love with learning while studying."
