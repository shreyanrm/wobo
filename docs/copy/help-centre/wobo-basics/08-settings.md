# Settings

**Everything you can change about Wobo lives on the You page, reached from the quiet "you" affordance in the top left.**

**Your class and your board**
Which class you are in and which board you follow, with the option of giving Wobo your own syllabus instead. Changing either changes what Wobo teaches from the next lesson on.

**Wobo speaks replies out loud**
On or off. It starts on, so a new learner hears Wobo without going looking for a switch, and one tap turns it off for good. The voice follows the language and country your board is in.

**Reduce motion**
On or off. With it on, everything happens in the same order and with the same timing, it just arrives without the movement.

**Appearance**
Auto, light or dark. Auto follows your device. In light, Wobo draws like marker on paper; in dark, like chalk on slate.

**Larger text and high contrast**
Two switches, each on its own. Larger text bumps the type size across the app; high contrast strengthens text and lines.

**Festivals we can wish you on**
Pick the festivals your family keeps, and Wobo sends a greeting on those days and no others. Pick none and Wobo sends none.

**Your data**
What Wobo remembers about you, and the erase that clears it. What the erase reaches, and what it does not reach yet, is written out in full on the privacy page below.

**The parent link**
Send a parent an invite to the Sunday note, see whether they accepted, and end the link in one tap. You can also look at the week the way a parent will see it before you send anything.

**Your plan**
Which plan you are on, what its day carries, the date it runs to, and Cancel. Cancelling takes two taps and a plan already ending can be put back in one.

**What is not here yet**
An accent picker, a sound-effects switch separate from the voice, a proactivity dial, a streaks switch, notification switches beyond the festival list, editing your name or your sign-in details, and hands-free listening. None of those is built, and settings will say so until they are. [Owner: this list is the gap between the settings the copy deck originally described and the rows the You screen actually has.
