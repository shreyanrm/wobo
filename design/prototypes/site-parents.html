02

Six things a great teacher does. Wobo does all six.

Not one of them is a setting to find or a button to press. They happen inside the lesson, on their own, whether or not anyone is watching.

It draws while it explains.

The answer arrives on a board, a line at a time, so your child watches the idea appear instead of reading a finished one. Where drawing is not the right form, the same answer becomes a short film, a thing to drag, or a voice talking it through.

It changes method after a second miss.

Never the same explanation again, louder. A worked example instead of a rule, the same idea drawn, an analogy from something your child already cares about, or talked out loud until they can say it back. It remembers which ones it has spent, so tomorrow starts somewhere new.

It teaches the ground first.

Before a topic that stands on older work, two or three short questions about that older work. If a piece of it is thin, that piece is taught as the opening of the same lesson, and every one of those questions can be skipped.

It does not move on until it stays learnt.

Reaching the end of a chapter is not the same as holding it. A chapter counts as done when what your child answered is still right the next time Wobo brings it back.

It brings back what slipped.

When something stops holding, it returns to your child’s board and names the topic that fell. The one moment a child is told they have gone backwards is spent saying which thing, and never on a grade.

It builds the example from their world.

If your child has told Wobo what they are into, the analogy is built out of that. If they have told it nothing, Wobo explains plainly rather than inventing a world for them.

The ground under this week’s chapter.

A child rarely falls behind on this week’s chapter. They fall behind on a chapter from two years ago that nobody ever went back for, and every chapter built on top of it costs a little more. So before Wobo teaches a topic that stands on older ground, it asks two or three short questions about that ground, and teaches whatever is thin as the opening of the same lesson.

Two different things, and they run on different clocks.

A doubt is cleared the moment it appears: after school, over the weekend, on a holiday, between classes, on the way home, wherever your child happens to be. Learning is the slower one, and it goes a bit at a time across weeks, so the week before an exam is revision rather than panic.

Wobo is built for the slower one on purpose. It will not mark a chapter learnt until it holds, it teaches the ground under a topic before the topic, and it brings back what slipped before it is lost. Every one of those costs a child time today and gives it back in March.

Nothing here is a shortcut. That is the whole design.

05

The questions parents ask.

Will it just give my child the answers?

No. Wobo draws the reasoning and asks your child to take the next step. When they’re close, it rings the gap on their answer and waits. Copying isn’t possible, because there’s nothing to copy until they’ve done the thinking.

Does it follow our school’s syllabus, or a generic one?

Your school’s. You pick the board and class when you set up, and Wobo teaches the chapter your class is on this week, in the order your textbook uses. If your school does something differently, tell Wobo and it adjusts.

Is it safe for a child to talk to alone?

Yes, and it’s built for exactly that. Wobo stays inside school subjects, has no opinions on anything contested, shows no ads, and never makes a child feel small. Voice isn’t stored. You can read the full detail on the Security and trust page.

Can I see what my child asks?

You see every lesson, practice set and the Sunday note. Questions word for word are shared only if your child allows it. We think that’s what a good tutor would do too.

What does it cost?

Nothing, every day, with a daily allowance of questions. Pro and Max raise that allowance. The plans page has the numbers for your country.

What happens if I stop paying?

Nothing renews, the plan runs to the end of the period already paid for, and then the account goes back to the free one, which never ends. Everything learnt stays exactly where it is. Cancelling takes as many taps as subscribing, and the screen that does it offers no discount, no pause and no reason picker.

Which subjects?

Every subject your board sets. The official chapter lists for CBSE, ICSE, ISC and NIOS are loaded; for any other board you hand over your school’s syllabus once and Wobo builds the plan from that.
