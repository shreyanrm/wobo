# donate-page

**Kind:** product surface, heywobo.com/donate
**To:** two readers at once — an adult who can pay for a place, and a family who cannot
**Governed by:** WOBO-PLAN §16 (the gift page, whose rules this page inherits), §14, voice.md §8

## OWNER CONFIRM — the four policy lines on this page

Everything else here is a product claim that can be checked against the app. These four are policy,
they are the owner's to set, and the page cannot exist without them. They are written below as
proposals, in the plainest form, so they can be approved or replaced line by line.

1. **Who gets a funded place.** Proposed: a family asks, and places open in the order they were
   asked for. No means test, no documents, no proof of income.
2. **What a donation buys.** Proposed: plans at the same price a parent pays. A donation is not a
   discount surface, exactly as a gift is not.
3. **Whether the donor learns who received it.** Proposed: no. The donor funds a place, not a child.
4. **Whether the family learns who paid.** Proposed: no. Nobody is asked to be grateful in public.

## The page

**Heading**
Buy a place for a child who cannot.

**Sub**
It is the whole of Wobo. Not a smaller version, not a trial, not the good parts locked. The same
subjects, the same tutor and the same everything a paying family gets.

**How it works**
1. A family asks. One line to us is enough. We do not ask for papers and we do not check up on anyone.
2. You fund a place, at the same price a parent pays, for the same length.
3. We open it for the next family waiting. You are not told who they are. They are not told who paid.

[Fund a place]

**What a place is**
A tutor that thinks out loud while it works. Ask about anything on the screen and the answer comes in
the form the idea needs: drawn on a board, played as a short film, built as a thing to drag, or
spoken. Then the practice, and a memory of what did not stick, so it comes back before it is lost.
Every number checked by code before it is shown. Their syllabus, their board, their class, in their
board's own words.

**What it is not**
Not a trial that runs out mid-chapter. Not a trimmed plan with the useful parts behind a wall. Not a
plan that ends in a sales call to a family who already said they cannot pay. No advertising is shown
to a learner because someone else paid for them, and nobody is asked to be grateful in public.

**If this is you**
If money is the only thing standing between your child and this, write to us. One line is enough.

[Ask for a place]

**The honest footnote**
A donation is not a subscription. Nothing renews and nothing is charged again. A funded place runs
the length that was paid for and then ends the way any plan ends, with everything they learnt still
theirs.

## Variables

## Rules
- **No counter, ever, until it is real.** No "1,240 places funded", no progress bar toward an invented
  target, no map of dots. A number on this page must come from the database or it must not be there.
- **No child on this page.** No photograph, no story, no first name, no composite "a girl in class 7".
  A donor funds a place, not a person, and a real child is never marketing material.
- **No pity, no urgency.** No countdown, no "only today", no language that implies a child is lost if
  the reader closes the tab. The ask is stated once, calmly, and the reader is trusted.
- **Both readers are equal.** The family asking for a place reads the same page as the donor, and
  nothing on it may make them feel like the subject of a transaction. Their section is not a footnote.
- **Nothing renews**, for the donor or the learner. Inherited from the gift page and not negotiable.
- **The price is the plan price.** A donation is not a discount surface.
