# Lines the owner wants on the public pages, and how they are set

## "Learning can be extremely fun and engaging" (owner, 2026-09-08)

The intent is right and it belongs on the public pages. The sentence as written is not how we say it,
for three reasons SELL.md and voice.md already hold: "can be" hedges a promise; "extremely" is the
intensifier every advert uses; "fun and engaging" is the category's own cliché, so it says nothing a
parent has not read on ten other sites. The same intent, said the way we say things, with a picture
behind each:

1. **"Learning, the fun kind."** Short enough for an eyebrow or a section head. Beside the climb, a
   bonus level and a boss.
2. **"Some of it is a game. All of it counts."** For the section that shows the arcade and the XP;
   it makes the promise and the honesty in one breath.
3. **"You will forget you are studying."** The boldest; for the landing's second chapter, beside a
   drawn answer landing on a page. It is puffery in the legal sense (docs/CLAIMS.md) and it is the
   line a fourteen-year-old would repeat.

Where: the landing page's chapter on the climb (one of 1 or 3), the for-parents page under the
"two modes" section (2), the plans page's eyebrow stays "Every subject · every board · free every day".
One line per section, never all three on one page (SELL.md: one job per page). The hero stays "Fall
in love with learning while studying."

## "Not a fixed course. It adapts to you and does not stop until the topic is mastered" (owner, 2026-09-09)

The owner's words: *"not a fixed course or content, adapts and changes to your learning style and pace,
and it doesn't stop until the topic is mastered."* The claim is true in the product and must stay so:
the re-teach ladder changes the explanation when one does not land (a drawing, a world, a worked
example, a simpler rung), the blueprint's levels are rendered against what this learner got wrong,
and the boss level holds the topic until it is held. So it is a promise we can make, and it separates
Wobo from every content library on the market in one breath. Said the way we say things:

1. **"Not a fixed course. A tutor."** The eyebrow; the shortest form of the whole idea.
2. **"No two learners get the same lesson. It changes to your pace and your way of thinking, and it
   does not stop until the topic is yours."** The section line; "yours" is our word for mastered, which
   a fourteen-year-old would not say.
3. **"When one explanation does not land, it tries another. And another. It stays until it lands."**
   The proof line, beside the re-teach ladder shown as three drawn attempts on one page.
4. For the parents' page: **"It is not content your child scrolls. It is a tutor that changes how it
   teaches until your child has it, and tells you when they do."**

Where: the landing's chapter after the drawn answer (2 with 3 as its proof), the for-parents page's
first promise (4), the eyebrow (1) on the landing's second chapter. Never the word "mastered" on a
learner-facing surface (voice.md: no school-report words); "mastered" may appear on the parents' page
and in the pitch to schools. One job per page still holds: this line does not join the fun line on the
same section.

Ships with: the landing and for-parents copy tests (`sell.test.ts`, `page-copy.test.ts`) updated to
hold the new lines, measured at 390 and 1440; queued right after wave 39 lands (WOBO-TASKS §10.47).
