**The law these three live under.** Uniform pricing. What varies by behaviour is the gift, the timing and the framing, never the number. Nothing here is addressed to a child: every one of these screens is for the adult who pays, and none of them may appear inside a learning surface. No countdown, no scarcity, no "last chance", no strike-through invented for the moment.
