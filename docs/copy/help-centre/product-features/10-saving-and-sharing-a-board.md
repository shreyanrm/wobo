# Saving and sharing a board

**Any board Wobo draws can be saved to your notes, or shared as an image.**

**Saving**

Say "save this" or use the save control on the plane or the full board. It goes to your notes with its title, the topic, and the date.

What is saved is the drawing itself, not a photograph of it. So a saved board still works: you can scrub its timeline back to watch it being drawn, move any slider on it, and ask Wobo about it later. Wobo remembers drawing it.

**Finding it again**

Notes is a tab on the lesson screen, beside the lesson itself. Your kept boards are listed newest first, with the date each was saved, and tapping one draws it again. There is no search and no grouping by subject yet, and the newest twenty-four are the ones kept: save a twenty-fifth and the oldest drops off.

**Sharing**

Share turns a board into a clean image with a small Wobo mark on it, ready to send to a friend, a group, or your family's chat app. The image is a picture; it does not carry your account, your progress or anything else about you.

**"Show mum what I just cracked"**

Ask Wobo and it will write the note for you, in its own words, and hand it to you to send. Wobo never sends it for you, and there is no way to push a single board down the parent link: the weekly note is the only thing that goes out on its own.

**Clearing them**

There is no delete on a single note yet. On You, under Settings, Your data, erase and start over clears the whole set along with everything else on the device.
