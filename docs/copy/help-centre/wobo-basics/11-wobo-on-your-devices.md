# Wobo on your phone, tablet and laptop

**Wobo works the same on any screen, with a finger, a mouse, a stylus or a keyboard, and everything follows your account.**

**Phone**
The full product in portrait. The plane arrives as a sheet from the bottom instead of a floating panel. Circling something is a finger drag. Holding to talk is a long press on the orb.

**Tablet**
Both orientations. If you have a stylus, use it: pressure changes the weight of your ink, your palm resting on the screen is ignored, and drawing your working next to Wobo's is the best way to use the whole thing.

**Laptop and desktop**
Keyboard shortcuts for everything, including hold to talk and the command palette, which reaches any action in the product. The plane is a floating panel you can move, resize, pin or shrink to a thumbnail with its ink intact.

**Screen readers and keyboards**
Every control is reachable and labelled, every practice question has a keyboard path, and everything Wobo draws has a text description that says what the drawing shows, not just that a drawing exists.

**Reduced motion**
Turn it on from You, under Settings, or let it follow your device. Everything still draws in the same order with the same timing; it simply appears rather than moves.

**Offline**
Lessons you have opened recently are kept on the device, so a dead network does not end a study session. Asking Wobo something new needs a connection. Anything you did offline syncs when you are back.
