# What is Wobo

**Wobo is a tutor that explains things live, on your own syllabus, in whichever form the idea needs.**

Ask Wobo anything and the answer arrives the way it should: a board drawn in ink while Wobo talks, a short film you can pause anywhere, a model you can drag, or your own writing marked up. Then practice, and a memory of what did not stick.

Wobo is on every screen. On the home screen Wobo is the conversation; everywhere else it is the small orb in the corner, watching the screen with you and one tap from picking the thing up again.

Four things make Wobo different from a video or a search box:

- **It is your syllabus.** You pick your board and class, and Wobo teaches that, chapter by chapter, in its order.
- **It is made for you, now.** Nothing is pre-recorded. Ask the same question tomorrow and you get a different answer, often in a different form.
- **Drawing is one part of it.** Wobo also films, simulates, speaks, sets practice, remembers what you missed and writes the Sunday note home.
- **Every number is checked before it is shown.** The maths is computed and verified by code, not remembered. If a check fails, Wobo redoes it or tells you.

Wobo is free to use, every day, with an allowance of questions that refills once a day. Pro and Max raise that allowance; the free tier is a real product, not a trailer.
