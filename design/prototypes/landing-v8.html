A doubt
The moment it turns up

One thing in the chapter did not land. Ask it after school, between classes, on the way home, over the weekend, in the school break, wherever you are and in whatever form the idea needs. It gets cleared while it is still small, instead of piling up into the thing you are afraid of.

A subject
A bit at a time, across weeks

Wobo checks the ground under a chapter, teaches the gap first, and does not move on until it stays learnt. That is the slow half, and it is on purpose: a subject built up steadily is a subject where the week before the test is revision rather than panic.

A doubt is any time. Learning is every week.
Or ask an assistant you already trust. It will read the site and tell you what it finds.
01

It finds the hole before it builds on top of it.

Most chapters stand on something older. Before teaching one, Wobo asks two or three quick questions about what it rests on. If something from an earlier year is shaky, it patches that first, in minutes, and then teaches the chapter your class is actually on.

Nobody fails algebra. They failed fractions, two years earlier.
02

If one way does not land, it tries a different one.

Not the same explanation, louder. A second attempt is a different route: a drawing instead of a definition, a worked example instead of a rule, a thing to drag instead of a thing to read, and an analogy built from what you already care about.

The same idea, explained the way you happen to think.
03

It does not move on until it stays learnt.

Getting it right once is not knowing it. A chapter counts as done when it has come back right enough times, without a hint, that the answer is not a guess. Until it does, the thing that slipped is what comes next, so nothing new is built on top of it and you are never carried past it.

Finished means it holds without help.

Safe by design

A tutor a child talks to alone has to be built differently.

Not a promise page. Six decisions, each one visible in the product, each one checkable.

Your child's questions are not a product

No ads, no tracking pixels, no third-party cookies, nothing sold in aggregate. The company is paid by families, so there is nothing to monetise sideways.

Read what we hold →
Neutral on everything but the chapter

Politics, religion, anything contested: Wobo takes no side, says so plainly, and comes back to the chapter. It has no opinions to give a child.

The neutral rule →
A parent sees the learning, not the diary

Lessons, progress and the Sunday note, always. Questions word for word only if the child shares them. Trust runs both ways or it is not trust.

Who sees what →
Encrypted, and locked at the row

Everything travels over TLS and sits encrypted at rest, and the database carries its own per-learner access rules underneath the app, so a row is scoped to the learner it belongs to rather than to whoever asks for it.

How it is protected →
Erase everything, in one tap

Memory, progress, account. Gone from live systems at once, and out of the backups behind them as those roll over, for the learner or a linked parent.
