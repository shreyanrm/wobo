# Conformance register — content and child safety

**Domain.** What Wobo *says* and *shows* to a child: model output screening, prompt injection,
correctness of teaching, honesty about uncertainty, reporting a bad answer, the UK Age Appropriate
Design Code, and the copy law in `DESIGN.md` §0 as it actually stands in the shipped app.

**Date.** 2026-09-04. **Branch.** `the-life`. **Commit at audit.** `0704817`.

**The one rule.** Nothing here is ticked without evidence. Every item is `MET`, `PARTIAL`,
`NOT MET`, `N/A`, or `NOT VERIFIED`. Evidence is a `file:line` or a command and its real output.
Where a claim is published to families and the code does not support it, the item says so plainly —
that is the point of the register.

**Commands run for this register**

```

$ bun test apps/web-pwa/src/screens/site/law-v5.test.ts apps/web-pwa/src/screens/law-v5.test.ts
```

gateway actually uses (`safety.py:104`).

## Reading the worst of it first

Four things in this domain would hurt a family, in order:

1. **The "quiet flag on every lesson" does not exist.** The help centre and an error screen both
   tell a child to flag a wrong answer. There is no flag control in the app and no endpoint that
   receives one (§6.1–§6.4).
2. **The deterministic safety screen is a fifteen-phrase keyword list** that misses abuse
   disclosure, eating disorders, grooming, non-English distress and trivial obfuscation, and
   fires the full crisis script at a civics question about suicide rates (§1.4, §2.5).
3. **The crisis helplines are hard-coded Indian numbers** served to every child in every country
   (`safety.py:109`), while `docs/legal/safety-and-content.md` §3 tells parents Wobo "names the
   emergency number for the learner's country" (§2.3).
4. **Age is asserted by the browser, never derived on the server**, and no memory or profiling
   capability is gated on consent — directly contrary to two published documents (§7.2, §7.7).

The register's genuinely strong half is correctness: the verified-number law on the board is real,
enforced server-side, and tested (§4). Do not let the gaps above obscure that.
