# Ink on your screen

**For a quick answer, Wobo draws straight onto whatever is already on your screen, and the ink fades when the point is made.**

This is the smallest of the three ways Wobo draws. A ring around the term you asked about. An arrow at the step where it went wrong. A number written beside a slider. One line, in place, on the thing itself.

**How it behaves**

- The ink is anchored to the actual thing, not to a spot on the glass. Scroll, rotate the phone, switch to dark mode, and the mark stays on its target.
- If the thing it points at disappears, the mark fades out rather than floating in space.
- It fades on its own once Wobo has finished the sentence. Nothing to dismiss.
- Wobo points before saying "this", so the ink is always there by the time the word arrives.

**When Wobo uses it**

For a pointer, a correction, one short line. If the answer needs more than a few strokes, a derivation or a diagram from nothing, Wobo brings out the plane instead.

**Asking for it**

You do not have to. But if Wobo has opened the plane and you would rather have the mark right here, say "here" and Wobo draws in place.
